Student Records Queries

Student Maxwell King Course COP 2937 Term Summer 2026

Week 8 · Submitted July 4

This assignment used a prebuilt student records database (Tutorial1) with three tables: Student, Courses, and Schedule, which links students to the courses they took and their grades. The work was writing five SQL queries against it. Most use aggregate functions, GROUP BY, and WHERE clauses that combine more than one condition.

Tables

TableFields
StudentStudentID, FirstName, MiddleInitial, LastName, DateOfBirth, GradePointAverage, Major, CreditsEarned, Probation, USCitizen, Class
CoursesCourseID, Title, Description, Credits
ScheduleScheduleID, StudentID, Course ID, Grade

Queries

1. Average GPA by Major

SELECT Major, AVG(GradePointAverage) AS AverageGPA
FROM Student
GROUP BY Major;

SELECT Major, SUM(CreditsEarned) AS TotalCredits

3. Business Majors Who Are Not U.S. Citizens

SELECT *
FROM Student
WHERE Major = "Business" AND USCitizen = False;

Returns two students: Todd Boyle and Ann Kidd.

4. Students on Probation Who Are Not U.S. Citizens

SELECT *
FROM Student
WHERE Probation = True AND USCitizen = False;

Returns one student: Todd Boyle, a Business major.

5. Total Credits for Students with a GPA from 3.0 to 3.5

SELECT SUM(CreditsEarned) AS TotalCredits
FROM Student
WHERE GradePointAverage BETWEEN 3.0 AND 3.5;

Returns 351 credits. BETWEEN is inclusive, so students with exactly a 3.0 or a 3.5 are counted.
